Sabeel Institute Kanban

Kanban is the task board used by staff and volunteers of Sabeel Institute. It is a private application for the institute's own team, not a public service.

Getting an account

Accounts are set up by an administrator at the institute — you cannot register yourself. If you need access, or you have signed in and are waiting to be approved, contact your administrator.

“This account isn't set up for the app yet”

The phone app signs in existing accounts only. Sign in on the website once first, then the app will work. Getting the app explains it.

Contact

Email privacy@oursabeel.com for anything about your account, your information, or deleting your account.
